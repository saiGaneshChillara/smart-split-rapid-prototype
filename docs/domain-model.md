## Group
A collection of users who share expenses together.

## Expense
An expense represents a real-world purchase or cost that needs to be shared among one or more members of a group.

## Expense contributor
The user who paid for an expense, an expense can have multiple contributors.

## Expense participant.
A user who share the expense, an expense can have atleast one participant other than the contributor if there is single contributor.

## Expense Item
An expense can have different items suppose if a group goes shopping shopping is expense and each item they bought is expense item.

## Split
Expense divided among the participants based on the items/portion of the expense they consumed.

## Settlement
The transaction where where expense participants pay the contributors the amount they owe, there can be partial settlements where they can pay any amount less than they owe and the amount they owe gets reduced.

## Refund
The transaction where the expense contributors get back their amount (comletly or partially) in case of events like cancellation.
